# Fun Workshop

Bilingual overview of Fun Workshop initiative and season sub-projects.

## About / 关于

Fun Workshop is a community-driven workshop series at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU) Taicang campus. We gather people who share interesting projects, ideas, and skills. It's a space for learning, sharing, and connecting across disciplines.

Fun Workshop 是西交利物浦大学（XJTLU）太仓校区的一个社区驱动的工作坊系列。我们聚集分享有趣项目、想法和技能的人们。这是一个跨学科学习、分享和连接的空间。

- **Main Website**: https://fun-research-workshop.github.io/fun-workshop/
- **Main Repository**: https://github.com/Fun-Research-Workshop/fun-workshop
