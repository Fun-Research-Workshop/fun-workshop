About Fun Workshop

关于 Fun Workshop

Fun Workshop is a community-driven workshop series at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU). We gather people who share interesting projects, ideas, and skills. It's a space for learning, sharing, and connecting across disciplines.

Each season features its own program of talks, workshops, and social events. Browse below to explore past and current seasons!
